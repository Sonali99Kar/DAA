# Design and Analysis of Algorithm (DAA) - Lab 09

**Topic:** Questions based on the greedy design paradigm (Difficulty level: medium and hard)

---

5. **[Candy Distribution Problem (Bi-directional Slope Greedy)]** $n$ children stand in a line, each assigned a rating. Each child must get at least one candy. Children with a higher rating than their immediate neighbours must get more candies than their neighbours. Find the minimum total number of candies needed. By choosing the proper input representation, write a program in C to validate your algorithm and derive the complexity analysis of your algorithm.
